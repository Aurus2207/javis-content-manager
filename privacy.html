Privacy Policy

Javis Content Manager (the "App"). Last updated: 27 October 2026.

1. Who we are

The App is a personal tool operated by an individual developer ("we") to manage the developer's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the general public.

2. Data we access

When the account owner connects TikTok through Login Kit and grants permission, the App may access:

  • Basic profile information (display name, avatar) via user.info.basic.
  • Account statistics (follower, like and video counts) via user.info.stats.
  • The list of the owner's own public videos and their statistics via video.list.

The App publishes a video via video.publish only when the owner selects a file and explicitly confirms the post.

3. How we use data

Data is used only to display channel performance to the account owner and to publish content the owner chose to post. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and retrieved data are stored only on the owner's own device or private server. We do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account is connected. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services, and can ask us to delete any stored data by contacting us below.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children and is not intended for users under 18.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
